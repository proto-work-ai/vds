Our Story

Crafting Dreams Into
Living Masterpieces

Founded in 2020, San Curtains was born from a singular passion — to bring the grandeur of luxury textiles into every home. We believe that a beautifully dressed window is the soul of an elegant interior.

From opulent velvet drapes to whisper-light sheers, every product in our collection is a masterpiece of texture, color, and craftsmanship — curated by our in-house design team and brought to life by skilled artisans.
