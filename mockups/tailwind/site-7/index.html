Day by Day

Your Itinerary

09 – 16 October 2026
7 Nights / 8 Days
Munnar
Thekkady
Kochi
Day 1  ·  09 Oct
Arrival & Onward to Munnar
Munnar

Airport pickup by your dedicated chauffeur. Begin the scenic journey to Munnar with refreshing waterfall stops along the mountain route. Check-in at Parakkat Nature Resort and settle into your valley-view suite.

✦ Waterfall Stops · Scenic Drive · Resort Check-in
Day 2  ·  10 Oct
Munnar Sightseeing
Munnar

Explore the finest attractions of Munnar: Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point, Kundala Lake with boating, and the famous Tea Museum. End with golden hour views over the mist-draped tea estates.

✦ Mattupetty Dam · Echo Point · Kundala Lake · Tea Museum
Day 3  ·  11 Oct
Leisure Day in Munnar
Munnar

A relaxed, unstructured day in the highlands. Enjoy the resort facilities, take a stroll through nearby tea gardens, visit local artisan shops, or simply unwind with panoramic mountain views.

✦ Leisure · Tea Garden Walk · Resort Amenities
Day 4  ·  12 Oct
Munnar → Thekkady Transfer
Thekkady

After a leisurely breakfast, check out and begin the scenic drive to Thekkady through lush spice-scented forest roads. Check-in at Greenwoods Resort cottage and explore the surrounding nature.

✦ Scenic Transfer · Forest Drive · Cottage Check-in
Day 5  ·  13 Oct
Thekkady – Wild & Spice
Thekkady

Periyar Lake boating safari for wildlife spotting. Guided spice plantation tour with tasting. Evening Kathakali or Kalaripayattu cultural performance. A rich immersion in Kerala's biodiversity and culture.

✦ Periyar Boating · Spice Plantation · Cultural Show
Day 6  ·  14 Oct
Thekkady → Kochi Transfer
Kochi

Post-breakfast check-out and drive through Kerala's scenic lowlands to Kochi. Arrive and check into the prestigious Crowne Plaza Kochi. Optional evening leisure along Marine Drive.

✦ Scenic Transfer · 5-Star Hotel Check-in · Marine Drive
Day 7  ·  15 Oct
Kochi City & Heritage
Kochi

Full-day Kochi sightseeing: Fort Kochi and iconic Chinese fishing nets, Mattancherry Palace, Jewish Synagogue, Spice Market, and the Dutch Palace. Sunset cruise on the backwaters.

✦ Fort Kochi · Heritage Sites · Backwater Sunset Cruise
Day 8  ·  16 Oct
Departure Day
Kochi

Enjoy a final breakfast at Crowne Plaza. Check-out and proceed to Kochi International Airport (or onward destination) with your dedicated chauffeur. Bid farewell to God's Own Country.

✦ Final Breakfast · Airport Transfer · Fond Farewell
