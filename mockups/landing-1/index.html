What We Offer

Six Ways We Can Elevate Your Space

Every service includes a complimentary in-home consultation, professional installation, and our satisfaction guarantee.

Custom DraperyFrom $289/panel

Floor-to-ceiling drapes tailored to your exact window dimensions. Choose from 200+ fabric options including velvet, linen, silk, and blackout weaves.

Roller & Solar ShadesFrom $149/window

Sleek, modern roller shades with precise light-filtering control. Solar shades reduce glare while preserving your view of the Pacific Northwest landscape.

Wood & Faux Wood BlindsFrom $199/window

Timeless warmth meets lasting durability. Real basswood and high-quality faux wood blinds resist humidity — perfect for Seattle's wet climate.

Motorized Smart ShadesFrom $449/window

Automate your window treatments with voice-control, app integration, and smart-home compatibility. Schedule sunrise/sunset scenes effortlessly.

Roman & Woven ShadesFrom $219/window

Soft, structured elegance that frames every window beautifully. Our Roman shades fold gracefully and woven woods bring natural texture to any room.
